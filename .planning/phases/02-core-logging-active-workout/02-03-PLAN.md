## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** lifter training in the gym, **I want to** log every set of my workout in a couple of taps and have it saved the instant I tap ✓, **so that** logging is fast, works offline, and never loses data even if the app crashes or my phone restarts.

> This plan completes the workout data layer: mid-session exercise management (LOG-04), ending a session by finish or discard (LOG-05), and the history queries (HIST-01/HIST-02) that the History tab and "start from history" flow read. Like Plan 02 it is DB-test-proven so the mutations are correct before any UI renders them.

<objective>
Extend `db/workouts.ts` with the remaining data-layer operations: reorder/replace/remove workout exercises (LOG-04/D-16), discard (hard-delete cascade, D-11) and finish (set finished_at, D-10), and the history read queries — chronological list, month-range for the calendar, and prior-bests for the finish-summary PR check. All covered by better-sqlite3 in-memory vitest.

Purpose: These are consumed by Plan 05 (exercise-management UI), Plan 06 (finish/discard + summary), and Plan 07 (History + start-from-history). Proving the tricky semantics first — especially replace-with-logged-sets (FLAG-1) and discard-must-hard-delete (Pitfall 2) — prevents silent history corruption.
Output: extended `db/workouts.ts` + extended test files, all green.
</objective>
