<objective>
Build the third vertical slice: a live-computed PR feed. After this plan, opening the Stats tab shows the most recent personal records across all exercises, recomputed fresh from the `sets` table on every focus (never stored — STAT-03/D-11), with a "See all" link to the full chronological history. PRs are StrengthLog-style rep-max records plus best estimated 1RM (D-08), produced by a single chronological pass modeled on the existing `prDetection.ts` convention but extended across all history.

Purpose: Make personal records visible and provably live — the never-cached guarantee is the core of STAT-03 and a locked PROJECT.md decision.
Output: `lib/records.ts` (+test) with `computeAllTimeRecords` and `computeExerciseRecordBoard`, `loadAllHistorySets` appended to `db/stats.ts` (+test), Section A of the dashboard, and the `app/stats/records.tsx` full-history screen.
</objective>

This plan delivers the live PR feed slice (STAT-03).
