## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** lifter, **I want to** log my body weight and body-part measurements with a date and see each metric's trend over time, **so that** I can track my physical progress alongside my training — free, offline, in one place.

*(MVP slice C — after this plan a user can define their own metric, e.g. "left ankle" or a second body-fat measure, and log/graph it like any built-in.)*
