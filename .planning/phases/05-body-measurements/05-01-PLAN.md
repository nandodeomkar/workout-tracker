<objective>
Build the pure-logic substrate the whole Body Measurements feature reads from: the built-in metric registry (D-01/D-03), the cm↔in converter (D-04/D-05), the one-point-per-date series reducer (D-12), and a shared range-cutoff helper extracted from Phase 4's chart screen (RESEARCH Open Question 2). Output: Four new `lib/*.ts` modules (+ tests) and a non-breaking refactor of the existing Stats chart screen to import the extracted range helper.
</objective>

## Phase Goal (user story)

**As a** lifter, **I want to** log my body weight and body-part measurements with a date and see each metric's trend over time, **so that** I can track my physical progress alongside my training — free, offline, in one place.

*(Derived from ROADMAP Phase 5 goal + success criteria "User can log body weight and body-part measurements with a date" / "User can view measurement history as trend graphs over time" + CONTEXT D-07. This plan delivers the tested math substrate the demoable slices in Plans 03-05 build on.)*
